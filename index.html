The Atelier

Twelve hands. No two settings quite the same.

Every ORNÉ piece passes through the same bench where it was drawn — wax carved, cast in recycled gold, and hand-set under a jeweler's loupe so each stone sits a fraction of a millimetre from where it was placed on paper.

We work in runs of twelve, never more, and retire a design once the run sells through. What's on the bench today won't be back.

MaterialsRecycled 18K & 14K gold, sterling silver
StonesTraceable diamonds, freshwater pearl, semi-precious
ProcessWax carve → cast → hand-set → polish
